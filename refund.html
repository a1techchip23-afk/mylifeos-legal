REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

My Life OS — Daily Planner & Goals

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. WHAT YOU PAY FOR

The app is free to use with limits. Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly) that unlocks unlimited goals, habits and skills, and Reports.

2. HOW TO CANCEL

You can cancel at any time in Google Play: Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > My Life OS > Cancel subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

3. REFUNDS

All payments are processed by Google Play, so refunds are issued by Google Play under its refund policy:

  • Within 48 hours of a purchase you can usually request a refund directly from Google Play (play.google.com > Order history > Request a refund).
  • After that, contact us at everyonweb@gmail.com with your Google Play order number. We will review your request fairly and in line with applicable consumer laws, and refund through Google Play where it is eligible.
  • Renewal charges you did not intend can be reported within the same channels; we will help you where we can.

4. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER CANCELLATION OR REFUND

Premium features stop at the end of the paid period (or immediately after a refund). Your data stays on your device and the free features keep working.
