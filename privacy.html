PRIVACY POLICY

My Life OS — Daily Planner & Goals

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. WHO WE ARE

My Life OS ("the app") is published by Laveti Uday Kiran (Everyonweb) ("we", "us"). Contact: everyonweb@gmail.com.

2. THE SHORT VERSION

We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter (tasks, expenses, goals, habits, notes, skills, budget) is stored only on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app stores the content you create in a local database on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (Settings > Backup data / Export transactions). Backup files are created in a location you choose and are yours to manage. Uninstalling the app or using "Clear all data" deletes the data stored by the app.

4. PERMISSIONS

  • Notifications: used only to remind you of tasks on their due date. Reminders are created on your device.
  • Run at startup: used only to re-schedule your task reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Billing (via Google Play): used only if you subscribe to Premium.

5. PAYMENTS

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that your subscription is active. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. CHILDREN

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. SECURITY

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and the app does not transmit it over the internet. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the app, so use a device lock. Backup files you export are not encrypted; store them safely.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Because we do not hold your data, you can view, export, correct and delete it directly in the app at any time. If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact everyonweb@gmail.com.

9. CHANGES

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available in the app (More > Privacy Policy) and the date above shows when it last changed.
